THE LISTENING ROOM

Music can speak. It can also make space.

01 · LISTENING

I trust the quiet parts.

Silence is not a gap I need to repair. Sometimes it is where a phrase becomes honest, and where the listener can finally hear themselves beside the music.

02 · ROOTS

The setar remembers.

I carry a Persian musical memory with me, but I do not want to place it behind glass. Tradition stays alive when it can breathe, change, and meet the present without pretending to be something else.

03 · PRESENCE

No need to rush the moment.

I am less interested in showing how much music can do than in discovering how little it may need. One note, fully heard, can already change the room.

A NOTE FROM THE ARTIST

“I do not want the music to explain everything. I want it to leave one window open.”

The work begins with attention: to the sound, to what surrounds it, and to what should not be forced. That is where the old and the new can meet without either one having to disappear.
