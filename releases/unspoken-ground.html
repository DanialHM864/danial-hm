Unspoken Ground

Some parts of us are clear long before they can be explained. This piece stays with that unnamed ground — memory, tension and recognition before they become sentences.

“Not every truth asks to become language.”

Rather than forcing a meaning onto the listener, the music leaves the door open. What appears there can remain personal.
