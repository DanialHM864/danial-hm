SINGLE · APRIL 24, 2026

They’d Charge for Air

A piece about the strange moment when value begins to swallow meaning. It does not shout at the world; it watches what happens when even the most basic things are measured, packaged and priced.

“Some titles ask the question before the music begins.”

The music stays restrained on purpose. Instead of turning the idea into protest, it leaves enough room for the listener to notice the tension for themselves.
