A Prayer Beneath the Ruins

Some prayers arrive after everything has been repaired. This one does not wait. The piece stays close to fracture, silence and endurance, without asking them to become beautiful before they are allowed to speak.

Rather than filling every moment, the music leaves room for what cannot be explained. The quiet is part of the composition; so is the listener.

“A prayer does not need the ruins to disappear before it can begin.”
