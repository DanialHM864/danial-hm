IN THE PRESENCE OF STILLNESS

Not an escape from life. A quieter way into it.

ALBUM · DANIAL HM RECORDS

In the Presence of Stillness

This album was built as a sequence rather than a collection of unrelated tracks. Each piece opens a different room, but the same question stays nearby: what becomes audible when we stop forcing an answer too quickly?

Stillness here is not emptiness. It is the space where sorrow can be felt without performance, where memory can settle, and where transformation can begin without pretending that everything has already healed.

The point is not to disappear into silence. It is to return from it with clearer ears.
