SINGLE · DECEMBER 26, 2025

Where Pain Learns to Bloom

This is not a piece that glorifies pain. It asks something quieter: after hurt has happened, can anything honest still grow around it?

“Growth does not justify the wound. It simply refuses to let the wound have the final word.”

The music stays close to that fragile space between damage and renewal, without forcing either one into a neat answer.
