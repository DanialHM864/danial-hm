50-WORD BIO

Danial HM is an Iranian composer and Persian setar artist based in Vienna. His contemporary instrumental music brings Persian musical memory into intimate ambient and cinematic spaces shaped by silence, restraint, resonance and attentive listening. He releases music independently through Danial HM Records.

100-WORD BIO

Danial HM is an Iranian composer and Persian setar artist based in Vienna. His work grows from Persian musical memory while remaining open to contemporary ambient, cinematic and instrumental language. He treats the setar as a living voice rather than a museum object, allowing tradition to breathe inside the present. Silence is part of the composition, not an empty space to be filled. His pieces often move with patience, leaving room for resonance, memory and the listener’s own presence. Danial HM releases his work independently through Danial HM Records.
