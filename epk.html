SHORT BIOGRAPHY

A Persian instrument, carried into the present.

Danial HM is an Iranian composer and Persian setar artist based in Vienna. His music is quiet without being distant: intimate, contemporary instrumental work shaped by memory, resonance and the space around each note.

He does not treat the setar as something preserved behind glass. For him, tradition remains alive when it can breathe, meet the present and speak in a personal voice without losing its roots.
